## Cleaning, Maintenance, and Spares

- **Build-plate cleaner** — dish soap and water are widely used for removable build plates. Isopropyl alcohol is useful for removing grease from new parts and cleaning motion components before relubrication.
- **Lubricant** — use the lubricant recommended for the specific component. Common options include [white lithium grease](https://amzn.to/49Kr3GD), [Super Lube](https://amzn.to/3xMq4bG), and [EP1 grease](https://amzn.to/4aXJOar).
- **Hotend socks** — inexpensive wear items that are worth keeping as spares
- **Thermal grease** — useful when replacing a heater or thermistor; verify that its temperature rating is suitable for the application
- **Threadlocker** — use the appropriate Loctite grade for the fastener and material. Nail polish is sometimes used as a light-duty alternative.
